Privacy Policy for NCF Calculator

Package ID: app.ncfcalculator.com · Effective Date: November 20, 2026

NCF Calculator (“the App”) is a Net Cash Flow calculator designed to help users compute Operating, Investing, and Financing cash flows, direct inflows/outflows, and multi-period cash projections. Your privacy is important to us.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal or financial data. All numbers, descriptions, and calculations you enter into NCF Calculator are processed entirely locally on your device in memory.

2. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

  • The App does not use analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking cookies.
  • The App does not sell, rent, or share user data with any third parties.
  • If you use the “Export CSV” feature, the generated CSV file is created locally on your device.

3. Children’s Privacy

NCF Calculator does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@ncfcalculator.com.
