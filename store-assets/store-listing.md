## Full Description (1,341 / 4,000 characters)
```text
NCF Calculator is a fast, clean, and private Net Cash Flow (NCF) calculator built for founders, analysts, accountants, and small business owners. Model your cash position in seconds—completely offline, with zero ads and zero data collection.

THREE CALCULATION MODES
• Activity Method (OCF + ICF + FCF): Break down Operating, Investing, and Financing cash flows with customizable inflow and outflow line items. Automatically computes category subtotals, total Net Cash Flow, and Ending Cash Balance.
• Inflows vs. Outflows (Direct Method): Track all cash receipts and cash disbursements side by side to see Net Cash Flow, Ending Cash Balance, and Cash Flow Margin.
• Multi-Period & Cumulative Schedule: Project inflows and outflows across multiple quarters, months, or years. View per-period NCF, running cumulative cash balance, and discounted Net Present Value (NPV) using your custom discount rate.

BUILT FOR REAL WORKFLOWS
• Live Formula & Visual Breakdown: See the exact NCF equation and proportional cash flow bars update as you type.
• Multi-Currency Support: Switch between USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), JPY (¥), CAD (C$), and AUD (A$).
• One-Tap Sample & CSV Export: Load a realistic sample scenario to explore, or export your calculation table directly to CSV.
• 100% Private & Offline: All calculations run locally on your device. No account, no tracking, and no internet connection required.
```
